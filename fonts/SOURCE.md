# GFF Latin

Downloaded from font resources referenced by Garena's official Free Fire Co-Lab website.

Official explanation of the GFF custom typeface: https://ff.garena.com/ffcolab/en/article/1/

Source CSS: https://cdn.wildflamestudio.com/common/web_event/colab_official/css/c915727.css and https://cdn.wildflamestudio.com/common/web_event/colab_official/css/83fad34.css

Regular: https://cdn.wildflamestudio.com/common/web_event/colab_official/fonts/GFFLatinW05-Regular.7b8bcd3.woff

Medium: https://cdn.wildflamestudio.com/common/web_event/colab_official/fonts/GFFLatinW05-Medium.c0d6224.woff

Bold: https://cdn.wildflamestudio.com/common/web_event/colab_official/fonts/GFFLatinW05-Bold.d2bc101.woff

These are official Free Fire web-font files. This does not independently establish that this exact family/version is the in-game Craftland widget font. No separate redistribution or commercial font license was provided by these pages. Included here to keep the user's local Free Fire concept mockups consistent and usable offline, not as a general font distribution package.
